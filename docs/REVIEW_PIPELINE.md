# Review Pipeline

Review is an independent evaluation of whether the task outcome satisfies its acceptance criteria and is supported by adequate evidence. The Reviewer did not produce the outcome under review.

## Gates

1. **Completeness:** the requested scope and evidence are present.
2. **Correctness:** the outcome satisfies acceptance criteria and relevant technical constraints.
3. **Risk:** material failure modes and regressions are understood.
4. **Traceability:** claims, artifacts, decisions, and observations can be linked to the task.
5. **Rework or acceptance:** findings are dispositioned explicitly.

The review result is Accept, Rework, or Blocked. Acceptance moves the task to Compliance; Rework returns it with findings; Blocked pauses progression and escalates. Review does not determine policy conformance or release authorization. See [DECISION_MODEL.md](DECISION_MODEL.md).
