# Human Intervention

The objective is minimum human intervention consistent with accountable governance. Delegation is the default for bounded, reversible, policy-conforming work.

Humans **must** intervene when:

- the objective, authority, or risk tolerance is materially ambiguous;
- AGF rules conflict or a policy exception is requested;
- a decision creates material irreversible impact or exceeds approved risk;
- compliance fails, evidence is insufficient, or a control cannot be verified;
- release affects a reserved environment, protected asset, or explicitly human-owned decision;
- a security, privacy, safety, or legal concern cannot be resolved within policy;
- the system cannot establish trustworthy task history, identity, or evidence;
- an escalation remains unresolved across accountable roles.

All other work should be delegated to the defined roles, with the Director coordinating and the Observer maintaining visibility. Human intervention records the question, decision, authority, rationale, constraints, and resulting task transition.
