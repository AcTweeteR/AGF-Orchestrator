# Code of Conduct

AGF-Orchestrator welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, employers, and technical preferences.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- give technically specific, actionable feedback;
- respect security embargoes and privacy;
- disclose material conflicts of interest when relevant;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or deliberate disruption;
- accept that governance and security constraints may outweigh convenience or speed.

Disagreement is expected, especially around autonomy, policy, safety, and architecture. Strong technical criticism is welcome when it remains focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, intimidation, disclosure of private information, coordinated disruption, or knowingly publishing unpatched exploit details against maintainer instructions is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect participants or the project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to behavior and may consider severity, repetition, intent, and security impact.

To report conduct that should not be handled publicly, contact the repository maintainer privately through the GitHub account that owns the project.
